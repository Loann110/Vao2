## Icons

The interface icons are kept in `frontend/global/vendor/`, downloaded by `scripts/vendor_icons.py`. Their licence files sit next to them.

- [Meteocons](https://github.com/basmilius/weather-icons) 2.0.0 by Bas Milius: weather icons (`meteocons.js`, `meteocons-LICENSE`).
- [Phosphor Icons](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core) 2.1.1: interface icons (`phosphor.js`, `phosphor-LICENSE`).
- Clothing icons (`clothing.js`, licences in `clothing-LICENSES`): [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide) and [Lucide Lab](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide-lab) (ISC), [IconPark](https://github.com/bytedance/IconPark) by ByteDance and [MingCute](https://github.com/Richard9394/MingCute) (Apache 2.0).
